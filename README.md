# Build an ADU

Model the effect of building an ADU on your property.

## Project Proposal

1. **The specific participants/community I want to engage are property owners and developers. Mostly homeowners looking to add an additional unit to their property. As people live and grow their households in a dense city like Philadelphia, they are bound to need more space. For example, fitting a car with a garage or a shed to fill their seasonal decorations/tools.**
   <!-- Describe the specific participants or community members you want to invite to participate. Be more specific than "the general public". -->

2. **The contributions/input I am inviting them to make are their desired size of ADU and whether they think its feasible on their property based off the auto-calculation. Their desired size of ADU will help my tool model the effect of building an ADU on a property. And, the result will collect the user's responses on whether they'd like to build after seeing the cost and its effect on their home's property value.**
   <!-- Describe what data, annotations, suggestions, or reports participants will contribute. -->

3. **The participants will benefit from contributing their input by modeling their ADU construction and assess its feasibility. It'll save them the time of calculating costs and benefits and allow them to come to a decision sooner.**

4. **The users that will use the collected data are housing developers to better understand how allotted space can change the overall profitability of a home. Planners can also benefit from this information by understanding the desirability of ADUs to inform policy.**

5. **The collected data will benefit those users by having a better understanding of spatial allocation. They are able to come to a decision easier and better informed when they understand the effects of allowing an ADU on house value. For planners, they'll be able to benefit by formulating a policy with residents' opinions in mind.**

## Overview
**This application is a tool for users to calculate the effect of building an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) on their own property. By simply inputting their desired ADU size and build type, the tool takes information based on construction costs and rent to determine whether it effects on their property value. **
