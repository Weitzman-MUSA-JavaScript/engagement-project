Which bus shelter would you like to suggest upgrades for?

Many SEPTA bus stops can be hard to immediately spot. Some sit on the shoulder of a suburban highway in the dark. Tell us which stops need a roof, a bench, a light, or a route map, and who should be part of the conversation.

  1. Find your stop. Search an address, or zoom in until the bus icons appear.
  2. Click the stop. Your cursor is a red ring. Click a stop and the ring locks onto it.
  3. Answer a few questions. No account or name needed.

    About this map

    StopScout collects rider suggestions for upgrades at SEPTA bus stops: roofs, benches, LED lighting, and route information, plus ideas for public art that reflects the neighborhood.

    Data: SEPTA Transit Stops (Spring 2026), SEPTA via DVRPC. Basemap © Mapbox © OpenStreetMap. Suggestions are contributed by the public through this map.
