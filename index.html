About the data

Store reports come from residents and community workers. They are not verified by the city, and no names, emails or addresses are collected.

  • Store locations: USDA Food and Nutrition Service, Historical SNAP Retailer Locator Data, stores authorized as of December 31, 2025, in and within 10 miles of Harris County.
  • Neighborhoods: City of Houston Planning and Development, Super Neighborhoods. Outside the city, U.S. Census Bureau 2020 incorporated and census-designated places, and the U.S. Geological Survey, Geographic Names Information System.
  • Census tracts and county boundary: U.S. Census Bureau, 2020 TIGER/Line.
  • Basemap: Esri World Light Gray Canvas, with data from HERE, Garmin and OpenStreetMap contributors.
